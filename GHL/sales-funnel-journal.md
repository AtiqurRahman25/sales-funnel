# Sales Funnel

A sales funnel is a step-by-step model that shows the journey people take from first learning about a business to buying a product. It is shaped like a wide funnel at the top because many people discover a brand, and it gets narrower toward the bottom because only a portion of them become customers.

## The Four Main Stages

### 1. Awareness

People **discover your brand** for the first time through channels such as:

- Advertisements
- Social media
- Search engines
- Word of mouth

**Goal:** Get your brand in front of potential customers and make them aware of your business.

---

### 2. Interest

People become **interested in your offer** and want to learn more. They may:

- Visit your website
- Read your blog
- Explore your products or services
- Research how you can solve their problem

**Goal:** Build interest by showing how your product or service can help them.

---

### 3. Decision

People are now considering whether to **buy from you**. They may:

- Compare your prices
- Evaluate features and benefits
- Read reviews or testimonials
- Compare your offer with competitors

**Goal:** Give potential customers enough information and confidence to choose your product or service.

---

### 4. Action

People take the final step and **purchase your product or service**. At this stage, a visitor becomes a customer.

**Goal:** Make the purchasing process simple, clear, and easy to complete.
