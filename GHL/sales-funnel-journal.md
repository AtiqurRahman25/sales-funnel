<h1>A sales funnel is a step-by-step model that shows the journey people take from first learning about a business to buying a product. It is shaped like a wide funnel at the top because many people discover a brand, and it gets narrow at the bottom because only a few final buyers make a purchase.</h1>

The Four Main StagesAwareness: People discover your brand through ads, social media, search engines, or word of mouth.Interest: People learn more about your offer, read your blog, or look at your website to see if you can help solve their problem.Decision: People compare your price and features with other choices and decide if they want to buy from you.Action: People pay for your product or service, turning them from a visitor into a customer
